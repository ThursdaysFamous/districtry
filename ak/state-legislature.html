Who is my Alaska legislator?

Alaska State Legislature district lookup — free, by address, ZIP, or a tap on the map.

← Back to the map

The Alaska State Legislature has 20 Senate districts and 40 House districts. Each Senate district is made of two whole House districts, and a district can split a borough, a city or a ZIP code, so the only way to know which pair covers an address is to put the address on the map. Senators serve four-year terms and representatives two-year terms.

Or leave it blank to open the map and pick your location there.

Find your Alaska State Legislature districts →

Opens the map with both chambers on. Search your address or ZIP, or tap your location.

What the lookup shows

Select any point and the two cards name the district for each chamber and the member who holds that seat, with their party, their e-mail and telephone, and a link to their own page on the Legislature's site.

Every other district covering the same point is listed beside them, so one click answers the state question and the county, city and federal ones together.

Two House districts to a Senate district

An address has one district in each chamber, and because every Senate district is two whole House districts, the House district decides the Senate one. Senate districts are lettered and House districts numbered. The tables below list both chambers in full, so a reader who knows their districts can look up the members without opening the map at all.

Where the names come from

Both rosters are re-read on a schedule from the legislature's own published member lists and land as a reviewed pull request; the map names no officeholder it cannot cite. A seat the source does not name is shown as unnamed rather than filled in.

Not for legal or official use. Boundary and roster data come from public sources that explicitly disclaim legal precision. Confirm district assignments and officeholders with the relevant government office before relying on them for anything official.
