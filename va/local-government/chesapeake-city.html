Chesapeake City Council

Every member of the Chesapeake City Council, as the Virginia Department of Elections' results name them.

Chesapeake City is an independent city, which belongs to no county, and the Chesapeake City Council is its governing body. Every member is elected at large, by the whole city. Richard W. "Rick" West is the city's mayor, elected by the whole city on 5 November 2024. Each name is the winner of an election in the Virginia Department of Elections' results, dated beside it. A result names who won, not who sits now, so a member appointed to a vacancy is not named until an election fills the seat. The map's County or City card reads the same file. The school board is a separate body, and the map's County or City card says who sits on it.
