Prince William County Board of Supervisors

Every member of the Prince William County Board of Supervisors, as the Virginia Department of Elections' results name them.

The Prince William County Board of Supervisors is Prince William County's governing body. Of the members this page names, 7 are elected from districts and the chair by the whole county. Each name is the winner of an election in the Virginia Department of Elections' results, dated beside it. A result names who won, not who sits now, so a member appointed to a vacancy is not named until an election fills the seat. The map's County or City and Supervisor or Council District cards read the same file. The school board is a separate body, and the map's County or City card says who sits on it.

districtry is an independent, unofficial civic reference. It never guesses at who holds a seat: every name above won an election in the Virginia Department of Elections' results or is listed on the county's or city's own website, and where neither settles a seat this page says so rather than filling the gap.
