Waynesboro City Council

Every member of the Waynesboro City Council, as the Virginia Department of Elections' results name them.

Waynesboro City is an independent city, which belongs to no county, and the Waynesboro City Council is its governing body. Of the members this page names, 4 are elected from districts and 1 at large. Each name is the winner of an election in the Virginia Department of Elections' results, dated beside it. A result names who won, not who sits now, so a member appointed to a vacancy is not named until an election fills the seat. The map's County or City and Supervisor or Council District cards read the same file. The school board is a separate body, and the map's County or City card says who sits on it.
