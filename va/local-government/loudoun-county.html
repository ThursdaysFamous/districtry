Loudoun County Board of Supervisors

Every member of the Loudoun County Board of Supervisors, as the Virginia Department of Elections' results name them.

The Loudoun County Board of Supervisors is Loudoun County's governing body. Of the members this page names, 8 are elected from districts and the chair by the whole county. Each name is the winner of an election in the Virginia Department of Elections' results, dated beside it. A result names who won, not who sits now, so a member appointed to a vacancy is not named until an election fills the seat. The map's County or City and Supervisor or Council District cards read the same file. The school board is a separate body, and the map's County or City card says who sits on it.
