Virginia sources & data layers

Every layer in districtry Virginia, and exactly where its data comes from.

← Back to the map 🧩 What data is missing?

This app is a reader over other people's data. It draws no boundary of its own and names no officeholder it cannot cite, so the useful question about any answer it gives is who published this, and when. That is what this page is for: the publishers the app credits as a whole, then one row per layer naming the source behind that layer specifically.

Not for legal or official use. Boundary and roster data come from public sources that explicitly disclaim legal precision. Always confirm district assignments and officeholders with the relevant government office before relying on them for anything official.

Sources last checked: October 29, 2026

Where a source publishes nothing, the app says so rather than guessing — the missing-data panel on the map is the standing list of what nobody publishes yet, and what would close each gap.

This page says where each answer comes from today.

Who publishes the data

The upstream publishers this app depends on, across all layers. Per-layer provenance is in the matrix below.

  • U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb County, city, magisterial district, town, school-division, ZIP, congressional and state legislative district boundaries.
  • unitedstates/congress-legislators U.S. House roster (CC0).
  • Senate of Virginia Virginia Senate members, their parties, e-mail addresses and session offices.
  • Virginia House of Delegates House of Delegates members, their parties, e-mail addresses and Capitol offices.
  • USGS The National Map Police, fire and EMS stations and post offices (public domain).

The layer matrix

16 layers — 5 political and legislative, 2 public safety, 3 schools, 6 geography and amenities. Each row names the publisher the boundary comes from, where the names on the card come from, and the ground the layer actually answers on — outside that ground the layer hides itself rather than guessing.

Supervisor or Council Districtsupervisor-district The district a county supervisor or city council member is elected from at the point, and who holds the seat. In Norfolk the point is in a ward and a superward, and the card names both.
  • Virginia Department of Elections — its statewide layer of election districts and precincts (Districts_Union_Simplified, layer 0), last changed 2026-09-22 and read 2026-10-30: 7,271 pieces joined into the 2,531 precincts they name, where two precincts claimed the same ground each part given to the precinct of the county it lies in, and checked to cover each county and city; pre-built by va/scripts/build_va_precincts.py and shipped with the app, drawn to within 12 metres. Available to anyone under § 24.2-103.1 of the Code of Virginia, as the Department told this project on 2026-10-30. A district is the union of the precincts the layer puts in it, keyed by the layer's district number except in Southampton County (by name, its numbers being stale), Poquoson and Waynesboro (whose council districts the layer records as precincts); a district name the layer gets wrong is corrected against the name the election results give it.
  • City of Norfolk — its two superwards (OpenData Maps, layer 8), from which two council members are elected; each Norfolk precinct lies at least 99.2% inside one, so each superward is drawn as the union of its precincts.
 va-local-officials.json, read from the Department of Elections' results of every local contest (historical.elections.virginia.gov) by va/scripts/build_va_local_officials.py: the winner of the latest election to each seat, the card saying which election and linking its result. A seat a special election has filled since is named from the special election. A member appointed to a vacancy is not shown until an election fills the seat, and where the results do not settle who holds a seat the card names no one and says why. Virginia — the 91 counties and 15 cities that elect their board or council by district. Arlington, Highland, Madison and Mathews counties and 23 cities elect at large; the layer is hidden there and the County or City card names the members.
Tribal Governmenttribal-government Whether this address is on tribal land, and which nation's government answers for it — Virginia has 3 such areas, held by the Pamunkey Indian Tribe, the Mattaponi Tribe and the Rappahannock Tribe.
  • US Census Bureau TIGERweb AIANNHA, current vintage — state reservations and off-reservation trust land, shipped with the app
 Each nation's council from that nation's own page, read weekly by scripts/tribal_council_scraper.py and dated on the card: the Pamunkey Indian Tribe's Chief, Assistant Chief and six council members (pamunkey.org) and the Mattaponi Tribe's Chief, Assistant Chief and seven council members (mattaponi.gov). The Rappahannock Tribe's own site names no council, so its card names only its Chief and Assistant Chief, from the Secretary of the Commonwealth's list of state-recognized tribes (also read weekly), and says the rest of its council is not named. The government, its seat and its website come from the Bureau of Indian Affairs' tribal leaders directory (data/bia-tribal-governments.json), except the Mattaponi Tribe's, which the Bureau does not list because the tribe is recognized by the Commonwealth and not by the federal government: its name, seat and website come from the Secretary of the Commonwealth's list of state-recognized tribes, and the card says so. The land is joined to the government by this project's own data/tribal-government-join.json. Virginia — the three pieces of land above, in King William, King and Queen and Essex counties. Every other point in the state is on no tribal land the Census draws for a government: Virginia's other recognized tribes hold no reservation or trust land the Census draws.

Geography & amenities 6 layers

The boundaries that place an address without electing anyone, plus the nearest public services.

LayerWhat it answersBoundary sourceWho it namesWhere it applies
County or Citycounty Which county or independent city contains the point.
  • U.S. Census TIGERweb — State_County, layer 1, pre-built for the state and shipped with the app; 133 features (95 counties and 38 independent cities, which the Census Bureau files as county equivalents), measured 2026-10-29. Simplified by Douglas-Peucker at a 5 m interval and refused over an 8 m fidelity ceiling.
 Who governs it: the board of supervisors or city council, the school board where it is elected, and a city's elected mayor, named from va-local-officials.json — the Department of Elections' results of the elections that seated them (historical.elections.virginia.gov), each member's card saying which election and linking its result. Where a body is elected by district the card names everyone and the district layers say which member represents the point. A member appointed to a vacancy is not shown until an election fills the seat, and where the results do not settle who holds a seat the card names no one and says why. Where a school board is appointed the card says so and names no one. The soil and water conservation district covering the county or city is named from the Department of Conservation and Recreation's list (dcr.virginia.gov), with the directors its voters elected from the same results, in va-conservation-boards.json; the two directors each board has appointed are not named. Virginia — statewide, all 95 counties and 38 independent cities.
Magisterial Districtcounty-subdivision The magisterial district containing the point and its county, drawn from the Department of Elections' current lines in the 72 counties whose census subdivisions are their election districts, and as the Census Bureau maps it elsewhere.
  • U.S. Census TIGERweb — Places_CouSub_ConCity_SubMCD, layer 1 (county subdivisions), read 2026-10-30 and pre-built by va/scripts/build_va_magisterial_districts.py, drawn to within 12 metres and shipped with the app: 552 features for Virginia, 513 magisterial districts in 94 counties, every one recorded with no government of its own; the 38 independent cities; and Arlington County, which the Census does not divide. Its names, and its lines outside the 72 counties below.
  • Virginia Department of Elections — its statewide layer of election districts and precincts (Districts_Union_Simplified, layer 0), through va-local-districts.json: in the 72 counties whose census subdivisions and election districts are one set of names (Adam's choice of 2026-10-22), each magisterial district is the Department's district inside the county's census ground, so its lines are the precincts' lines, drawn to within 12 metres. Available to anyone under § 24.2-103.1 of the Code of Virginia, as the Department told this project on 2026-10-30.
 NO OFFICEHOLDER, AND THE CARD SAYS WHY. A magisterial district has no government of its own. In the 72 counties drawn from the Department's lines the card says the district is the one the board of supervisors is elected from and points to the Supervisor or Council District card, which names the member; in the 19 counties whose election districts differ from the Census's copy it says the lines are not the election districts; in Highland, Madison and Mathews it says the board is elected at large. Which bodies a county elects by district is read from va-local-officials.json. Virginia — statewide, all 552 records.
ZIP Codezip-code The ZIP Code Tabulation Area covering the point — the Census Bureau's areal approximation of the USPS delivery routes sharing a ZIP code, not a postal boundary.
  • U.S. Census TIGERweb — ZIP Code Tabulation Areas, layer 11 of the PUMA/TAD/TAZ/UGA/ZCTA service, copied into a tile archive served from this site, re-fetched from the Census weekly. ZCTAs carry NO STATE FIELD because they cross state lines, so this is the one TIGERweb layer here fetched by a bounding ENVELOPE rather than STATE='51'. 2,609 features in Virginia's box, measured 2026-10-29.
 NO OFFICEHOLDER, AND NONE IS MISSING: a ZIP code is a mail-routing convenience, not a unit of government, so nobody is elected from one. The card says what the boundary actually is rather than leaving a reader to assume it means more. Virginia. The layer is hidden outside the state outline, because outside it the Census's point query answers with a neighbouring state's real ZIP code.

About this page

This page is generated from the same list of layers the app itself is built from, so a layer cannot ship without a row here and a row here cannot outlive its layer. If a source above has moved, gone dark, or is simply wrong, that is a bug worth reporting.

Found a source the app is missing? Every accepted one is credited by name in SOURCE_CREDITS.md. There's no cash bounty — the credit is the reward.
