# WATCH.md — Virginia's watch calendar and deferred measurements

Two things live here: *when to look* for boundary and roster changes in this instance's
sources, and the measurements this instance has taken but not yet acted on. The repo's
`docs/REDISTRICTING_RUNBOOK.md` is *what to do* when a boundary changes. Update the
"Last done" column each time you complete a row — a checkpoint with a stale date is a
checkpoint that didn't happen.

The plan this instance is built to is the Virginia plan of 2026-10-21 (claude.ai artifact
R4apNobGbZaZRpCDiWRxVc), with Adam's answers of 2026-10-22: all three choices as
recommended. It is built in five changes; this file was written with the first and updated
with the second.

1. **Hidden: the statewide layers** (merged 2026-10-30). Congress, both chambers with their
   members, counties and cities, towns, school divisions, ZIP codes, the census's county
   subdivisions, and the outline of Virginia.
2. **Hidden: local districts and names** (the change that added this sentence). Supervisor,
   council and school board districts and all 2,531 precincts from the Department of
   Elections' statewide layer, with names from the state's election results, each card saying
   which election the name comes from.
3. **Go live**, on Adam's word only.
4. **Soil and water conservation districts and tribal land**, each a change of its own. The
   conservation districts came first (the change that added this sentence): the County or City
   card names the district a county or city is in, from the Department of Conservation and
   Recreation's list of the 47 districts, and the directors its voters elected, from the 2023
   results. The two appointed directors of each district are on no list this app reads and are
   not named. Tribal land came second (the change that added this sentence): the Tribal
   Government layer draws the Pamunkey and Mattaponi state reservations and the Rappahannock
   Trust Land, and names the Pamunkey's and the Mattaponi's councils from each nation's own
   page. The Rappahannock Tribe's own site names no council, so its card names nobody and says
   why (gap `va-tribal-government`).
5. **County subdivisions from the Department's lines** where the census names match.
