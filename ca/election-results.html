Unofficial results of San Francisco's November 3, 2026 consolidated general election, as the San Francisco Department of Elections publishes them. Each race lists every choice's votes and share of the vote. The map colours each precinct by who got the most votes there.

Unofficial results. The Department adds the ballots it counts each day after the election and certifies its results within 30 days. In a ranked-choice contest these are first choices; the winner comes from the Department's ranked-choice count. These numbers are not certified and will change. About these results

These pages and the map read the same files, rebuilt each day from the publishers' own results until they are certified. A race's totals here are the publisher's; where the publisher reports a race by precinct, the map shows the same race one precinct at a time. A race is listed under the kind of office it elects, in the order the map's race menu lists them.

Not for legal or official use. These are the results as published on the dates above, and certified results can differ from them. For the official count, ask the office that ran the election.
