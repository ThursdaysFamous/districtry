22A.010 Numbers of judges -- Districts.
(1)   The Court of Appeals shall consist of fourteen (14) judges, with two (2) judges
      from each of seven (7) Court of Appeals districts.
(2)   The districts of the Court of Appeals shall correspond in geographical dimensions to
      the districts of the Supreme Court, as defined in KRS Chapter 21A.
               Effective: March 23, 1976
               History: Created 1976 Ky. Acts ch. 70, sec. 2, effective March 23, 1976.
