(2)   If any precinct or part of a precinct that is part of Jefferson County School District
      is not identified in subsection (1) of this section, the county board of elections shall
      add that territory to a contiguous division. All actions taken by the county board of
      elections under this subsection shall be to ensure that minimal changes are made
      that allow the divisions to fall within a five percent (5%) population deviation.
