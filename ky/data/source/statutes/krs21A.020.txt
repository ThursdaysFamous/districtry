21A.020 Election of justices, when held.
Elections for Justices of the Supreme Court shall be held at the regular elections, and
every eight (8) years thereafter, as follows:
(1) In the fifth district, in 1976;
(2) In the third district, in 1978;
(3) In the seventh district, in 1980; and
(4) In the first, second, fourth and sixth districts, in 1982.
              Effective: March 23, 1976
              History: Created 1976 Ky. Acts ch. 67, sec. 3, effective March 23, 1976.
