Who is my Idaho legislator?

Idaho Legislature district lookup — free, by address, ZIP, or a tap on the map.

← Back to the map

The Idaho Legislature is elected from 35 districts. Each district elects one senator and two representatives, so one district number answers for both chambers. A district can cross county lines and split a ZIP code, so the only way to know which one covers an address is to put the address on the map. Senators and representatives both serve two-year terms.

Or leave it blank to open the map and pick your location there.

Find your Idaho Legislature districts →

Opens the map with both chambers on. Search your address or ZIP, or tap your location.

What the lookup shows

Select any point and the two cards name its district number, the senator and the two representatives it elects, with their party, their e-mail and telephone, and a link to their own page on the Legislature's site.

Every other district covering the same point is listed beside them, so one click answers the state question and the county, city and federal ones together.

One map, three members

An address has one legislative district, and it answers for both chambers: its senator and its two representatives each represent every point in it. The tables below list both chambers in full, so a reader who knows their district number can look up all three members without opening the map at all.

Where the names come from

Both rosters are re-read on a schedule from the legislature's own published member lists and land as a reviewed pull request; the map names no officeholder it cannot cite. A seat the source does not name is shown as unnamed rather than filled in.

Not for legal or official use. Boundary and roster data come from public sources that explicitly disclaim legal precision. Confirm district assignments and officeholders with the relevant government office before relying on them for anything official.
