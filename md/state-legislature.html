Who is my Maryland legislator?

Maryland General Assembly district lookup — free, by address, ZIP, or a tap on the map.

← Back to the map

The Maryland General Assembly has 47 Senate districts and 71 House of Delegates districts. Each Senate district elects three delegates, either all together or from two or three subdistricts drawn inside it, and a district can split a county, a city or a ZIP code, so the only way to know which pair covers an address is to put the address on the map. Senators and delegates both serve four-year terms.

Or leave it blank to open the map and pick your location there.

Find your Maryland General Assembly districts →

Opens the map with both chambers on. Search your address or ZIP, or tap your location.

What the lookup shows

Select any point and the two cards name the district for each chamber and the senator and delegates who hold its seats, with their party, their offices and a link to their own page on the General Assembly's site.

Every other district covering the same point is listed beside them, so one click answers the state question and the county, city and federal ones together.

Three delegates to a Senate district

An address has one district in each chamber, and because every House of Delegates district is a Senate district or a subdistrict drawn inside one, the House district decides the Senate one. The tables below list both chambers in full, so a reader who knows their district can look up the members without opening the map at all.

Where the names come from

Both rosters are re-read on a schedule from the legislature's own published member lists and land as a reviewed pull request; the map names no officeholder it cannot cite. A seat the source does not name is shown as unnamed rather than filled in.

Not for legal or official use. Boundary and roster data come from public sources that explicitly disclaim legal precision. Confirm district assignments and officeholders with the relevant government office before relying on them for anything official.
