APPLICATION FOR SOUTHWEST DISTRICT COUNCIL SEAT
Purpose
Use this form to apply for consideration for appointment to fill the unexpired term for the Southwest District seat on the Hutchinson City Council. The appointed Councilmember will serve through December 2027. An election for the Southwest District seat will be held in November 2027, at which time the appointed Council member may choose to run for election. The candidate elected by voters will serve the remainder of the district's current term, through December 2029.
