GOVERNING BODY-REGULAR SESSION
About: Leawood operates under a Mayor/Council form of government, with a separately elected Mayor and eight council members. Collectively, these nine individuals make up the city's Governing Body.
Member Terms: Council members from four wards are elected on a nonpartisan basis, and all governing body members are elected to staggered four-year terms.
