    See your county, city, village or town, school districts, Wisconsin Senate and Assembly districts, ZIP code, and U.S. House district — plus who represents you.

    Not for legal or official use. Boundary and roster data come from public sources that disclaim legal precision — confirm with the relevant government office before relying on them for anything official.

    Independent and unofficial. districtry is not a government service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any government agency. It asks for an address only to place a point on the map — there is no account, no sign-in, and it never asks for personal or financial information. Seals shown beside a district belong to that body and indicate whose district you are looking at, not any endorsement of this site. Built and run by Adam Overberg.

    What data is missing

    This app only shows what a government actually publishes. Where a district or an officeholder is missing, it is nearly always because nobody publishes it, or because the publisher won't let a program read it — not because the code isn't written. Each line below says what you won't see and where; open one to find out why, and what would fix it.
