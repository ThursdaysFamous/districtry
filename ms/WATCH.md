# WATCH.md — Mississippi's watch calendar and deferred measurements

Two things live here: *when to look* for boundary and roster changes in this instance's
sources, and the measurements this instance has taken but not yet acted on. The repo's
`docs/REDISTRICTING_RUNBOOK.md` is *what to do* when a boundary changes. Update the
"Last done" column each time you complete a row — a checkpoint with a stale date is a
checkpoint that didn't happen.

---

|---|---|---|---|

| Monthly (1st, 20:13 UTC) | Source freshness for every dataset this instance depends on, including a count of the five districts on each of the eight county maps the supervisor layer is drawn from | `.github/workflows/ms-validate-sources.yml` → tracking issue on WARN/FAIL | Read the issue; the job stays green on purpose. | Once a year, when TIGERweb's current layers move to a new vintage | Which counties' supervisor district lines the Census now carries current | `python3 ms/scripts/build_ms_supervisor_districts.py --survey` (writes nothing; it reads every county's 2020 census blocks from TIGERweb) | A county marked `NOT LISTED` newly passes both tests: look at it, add it to `CENSUS_COUNTIES` and rebuild. A rebuild against a new vintage stops until `CURRENT_VINTAGE` and the card's wording are moved together, which `--check` holds. A county drawn from its own map whose Census lines have changed stops the build too: compare the Census's new lines with the county's, and if they agree move the county from `COUNTY_SOURCES` to `CENSUS_COUNTIES` |
| After the general election of 2 November 2027 | Every state senator, state representative and county supervisor | The Secretary of State's own election calendar in the 2026 directory lists all three among the offices on that ballot | Check the weekly legislature refresh picks up the new members once the legislature's gallery lists them, and that the next edition of the directory names the new boards; a county whose supervisor districts are drawn here should be re-read for new lines after the 2030 census, not before |
