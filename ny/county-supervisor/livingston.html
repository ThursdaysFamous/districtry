Livingston County Board of Supervisors

Every member of the Livingston County Board of Supervisors, from the county's own published roster.

The Livingston County Board of Supervisors is elected by district. This page lists 17 districts and the 17 members who hold them, exactly as the county publishes them — it is the same roster the map's board of supervisors card reads.
