Saratoga County Board of Supervisors

Every member of the Saratoga County Board of Supervisors, from the county's own published roster.

The Saratoga County Board of Supervisors is elected by district. This page lists 21 districts and the 23 members who hold them, exactly as the county publishes them — it is the same roster the map's board of supervisors card reads.
