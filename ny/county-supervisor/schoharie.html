Every member of the Schoharie County Board of Supervisors, from the county's own published roster.

The Schoharie County Board of Supervisors is elected by district. This page lists 16 districts and the 16 members who hold them, exactly as the county publishes them — it is the same roster the map's board of supervisors card reads.
