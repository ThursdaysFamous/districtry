Wake County Board of Commissioners

Who won each seat on the Wake County Board of Commissioners at its most recent election, from the North Carolina State Board of Elections' results.

These are the people who won each seat on Wake County's Board of Commissioners at its most recent election in the North Carolina State Board of Elections' results, each dated to that election. They are election winners, not a list the county keeps up to date: a board fills a vacancy by appointment, so a seat that has changed hands since may still show the person who won it.
