Who is running in my ward? Chicago 2027 candidates

Everyone who filed for Chicago's February 23, 2027 election: mayor, clerk, treasurer, all 50 wards and the police district councils.

← Back to the map

Filed, objections pending. These are the 253 people who filed to run in Chicago's municipal election on Tuesday, February 23, 2027: for mayor, clerk and treasurer, for alderperson in each of the 50 wards, and for the 22 police district councils. The list is the Chicago Board of Election Commissioners' own, as of 4:01 p.m. on November 9, 2026.

83 of them are marked Challenged: someone has filed an objection to their nominating petitions, and the Board decides each objection before it certifies the ballot, which it must do by December 17, 2026. This page follows the Board's list as it changes.

About this list

The names, their order and each status are the Chicago Board of Election Commissioners', from its candidate list as of 4:01 p.m. on November 9, 2026, linked from its Candidates page. The Board titles the list "in ballot order". Names are printed in capitals, as the Board prints them. The Board's list also gives each candidate's home address; this page leaves it out.

What each status means:

  • Candidate: The Board lists this filing with no objection on file.
  • Challenged: Someone has filed an objection to the candidate's nominating petitions. The Board hears each objection and decides whether the name goes on the ballot.

The Board must certify the names that go on the ballot by December 17, 2026, 68 days before the election (10 ILCS 5/10-15).

Not the official ballot. This is the Board's list as it stood at 4:01 p.m. on November 9, 2026. Names can come off it before the ballot is certified. For the official list, ask the Chicago Board of Election Commissioners.
