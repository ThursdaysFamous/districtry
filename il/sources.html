Illinois sources & data layers

Every layer in districtry Illinois, and exactly where its data comes from.

← Back to the map 🧩 What data is missing?

This app is a reader over other people's data. It draws no boundary of its own and names no officeholder it cannot cite, so the useful question about any answer it gives is who published this, and when. That is what this page is for: the publishers the app credits as a whole, then one row per layer naming the source behind that layer specifically.

Not for legal or official use. Boundary and roster data come from public sources that explicitly disclaim legal precision. Always confirm district assignments and officeholders with the relevant government office before relying on them for anything official.

Data last verified: September 11, 2026

Where a source publishes nothing, the app says so rather than guessing — the missing-data panel on the map is the standing list of what nobody publishes yet, and what would close each gap.

This page says where each answer comes from today. What has changed here since the app launched, and when, is its own record: the history page.

Who publishes the data

The upstream publishers this app depends on, across all layers. Per-layer provenance is in the matrix below.

  • OpenStreetMap contributors (Nominatim & Photon/Komoot) Address search and the office-address pins.
  • Chicago Data Portal The city's own open-data portal — wards, precincts, community areas, CPS zones and networks, libraries.
  • U.S. Census TIGERweb The national boundary service behind the statewide layers: counties, townships, municipalities, school districts, ZIP code areas, and the legislative districts.
  • Cook County GIS The Clerk's tax-agency tilings — commissioner districts, judicial subcircuits, fire/park/library districts, TIFs, suburban precincts.
  • unitedstates/congress-legislators The public-domain roster of sitting members of Congress, including district and D.C. offices.
  • Illinois General Assembly The Illinois Senate and House member rosters.
  • Illinois State Board of Elections The county clerk and election authority directory.
  • School board district map (ilsenateredistricting.com) The ERSB 20-subdistrict shapefile (SB 15) behind Chicago's elected school board districts.
  • IL Supreme Court districts (PA 102-0011 shapefile) The judicial district boundaries as enacted.
  • Cook County Board of Review (PA 102-0012 shapefile) The three Board of Review districts, and the commissioners holding them.
  • Chicago Police Department (ArcGIS feature services) Police district and beat boundaries, station locations, and the school-location layer. The district commander and CAPS contacts are scraped weekly from chicagopolice.org itself.
  • USGS The National Map The national structures dataset behind the nearest police station, fire station and post office.
  • Chicago Board of Elections Early voting sites and secured drop boxes, transcribed per election.
  • County and municipal governments across Illinois Dozens of individual county GIS services, board pages and clerk directories — each named in the row of the layer it feeds, and tracked per county in COUNTY_STATUS.md.

The layer matrix

40 layers — 11 political and legislative, 7 public safety, 9 schools, 13 geography and amenities. Each row names the publisher the boundary comes from, where the names on the card come from, and the ground the layer actually answers on — outside that ground the layer hides itself rather than guessing.

Library Districtlibrary-district The public library district serving the point, and whether library service there comes from a district or a municipal fund.
  • Cook County GIS — political boundaries, layer 20 (library tax district) and layer 19 (library fund)
  • Each county's own GIS or election service, county by county
  • Pre-built from the county's published district map, for the counties that publish no vector data
  • Illinois Broadband Office / Connected Nation, IL_Boundary_Layers layer 11 — Carroll, Lee, Macoupin, Randolph, Sangamon, St. Clair and Stephenson ONLY. Not a county or library publication: every attribute besides the library name and type is a broadband service metric. Its licence (AGOL item 675906ae06b5460296154760b7fb1367) permits public use, disclaims warranty, says the data is NOT for legal boundary determinations, and requires both attribution and disclosure of any changes — so each of those cards names this publisher AND states that the boundary shown was clipped to the county and simplified to 5 m.
  • Which county feeds which entry, county by county (COUNTY_STATUS.md)
 Four sources, all rebuilt weekly by CI. The district-governed libraries that file an Annual Financial Report with the Illinois Comptroller name their own board and administration on it (il-library-district-officials.json). Every library's own page in the Illinois library systems' shared directory names one administrator and links the library's site (il-library-contacts.json); it names no trustee, because that directory's staff lists and annual certifications are behind a sign-in. For the cards no filing answers for — the municipal and township libraries — a board of trustees is read off the library's OWN website, each record naming the page it came from and the day it was read (il-library-trustees.json). Where a county's GIS carries trustees or a district office the card shows them, and Cicero's library board comes from the Cook County Clerk's directory of elected officials (cook-library-trustees.json). The counties that publish a library-district layer, plus seven that publish none and are drawn from a statewide layer instead.

Special Service Areassa Whether the point sits inside a Special Service Area — a local property-tax district funding street cleaning, landscaping, security, marketing and façade programs beyond the city's baseline service — and which one.

The city's boundary dataset names neither, so the card names nobody and links the city's SSA program page. The City of Chicago — and most points in the city are in no SSA at all.

About this page

This page is generated from the same list of layers the app itself is built from, so a layer cannot ship without a row here and a row here cannot outlive its layer. If a source above has moved, gone dark, or is simply wrong, that is a bug worth reporting.

Found a source the app is missing? Every accepted one is credited by name in SOURCE_CREDITS.md. There's no cash bounty — the credit is the reward.
