Which school board district am I in?

Chicago's elected Board of Education districts — free lookup by address, ZIP, or a tap on the map.

← Back to the map

Chicago's Board of Education seats one member for each sub-district. The city has ten school board districts, each split in two, 1a through 10b. The map shows the sub-district for any address, which is how the ballot and the Board name the seat.

What the lookup shows

Select any point in Chicago and the school board card shows the elected school board sub-district that covers it and the board member currently holding the seat — taken from the official CPS roster. If a seat is vacant, the card says so rather than guessing.

School board districts are separate from CPS attendance zones — the map also has layers for the elementary and high school attendance boundaries, under Schools.

Where the boundaries come from

The district geometry comes from the official elected-school-board sub-district shapefile, and the member roster from CPS's published district roster; every layer's exact source is on the sources & data layers page. The map is free, requires no login, and sets no tracking cookies.

Not for legal or official use. Boundary and roster data come from public sources that explicitly disclaim legal precision. Confirm district assignments and officeholders with the relevant government office before relying on them for anything official.
