Who is my North Dakota legislator?

North Dakota Legislative Assembly district lookup — free, by address, ZIP, or a tap on the map.

← Back to the map

The North Dakota Legislative Assembly is elected from 47 districts. Each district elects one senator and two representatives, so one district number answers for both chambers, and where a district is split into halves, A and B, each half elects one of the two. A district can cross county lines and split a ZIP code, so the only way to know which one covers an address is to put the address on the map. Senators and representatives both serve four-year terms.

Or leave it blank to open the map and pick your location there.

What the lookup shows

Select any point and the two cards name its district, the senator and the representatives it elects, with their party, their e-mail, their telephone where the Legislative Assembly lists one, and a link to their own page on its site.

Every other district covering the same point is listed beside them, so one click answers the state question and the county, city and federal ones together.

One map, three members

An address has one legislative district, and it answers for both chambers: its senator represents every point in it, and so do its two representatives, or, where the district is split into halves, the one elected from the reader's half. The tables below list both chambers in full, so a reader who knows their district can look up its members without opening the map at all.

Where the names come from

Both rosters are re-read on a schedule from the legislature's own published member lists and land as a reviewed pull request; the map names no officeholder it cannot cite. A seat the source does not name is shown as unnamed rather than filled in.

Not for legal or official use. Boundary and roster data come from public sources that explicitly disclaim legal precision. Confirm district assignments and officeholders with the relevant government office before relying on them for anything official.
