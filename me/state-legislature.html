Who is my Maine legislator?

Maine Legislature district lookup — free, by address, ZIP, or a tap on the map.

← Back to the map

The Maine Legislature has 35 Senate districts and 151 House districts. Both are drawn over the same ground and a district can split a county, a city or a ZIP code, so the only way to know which pair covers an address is to put the address on the map. Senators and representatives both serve two-year terms.

Or leave it blank to open the map and pick your location there.

Find your Maine Legislature districts →

Opens the map with both chambers on. Search your address or ZIP, or tap your location.

What the lookup shows

Select any point and the two cards name the district number for each chamber and the member who holds that seat, with their party, their e-mail, their telephone where the Legislature lists one, and a link to their own page where it publishes one.

Every other district covering the same point is listed beside them, so one click answers the state question and the county, city and federal ones together.

Two chambers, two maps

An address has one district in each chamber and the two rarely share a boundary: the chambers are apportioned separately, and in several of these states one chamber's districts are built by pairing the other's. The table below lists both in full, so a reader who knows their district number can look up the member without opening the map at all.

Where the names come from

Both rosters are re-read on a schedule from the legislature's own published member lists and land as a reviewed pull request; the map names no officeholder it cannot cite. A seat the source does not name is shown as unnamed rather than filled in.

Not for legal or official use. Boundary and roster data come from public sources that explicitly disclaim legal precision. Confirm district assignments and officeholders with the relevant government office before relying on them for anything official.
