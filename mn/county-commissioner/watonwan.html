These are the people who won each seat on Watonwan County's Board of Commissioners at its most recent election in the Minnesota Secretary of State's results, each dated to that election. They are election winners, not a list the county keeps up to date, so a seat that has changed hands since, by a resignation or an appointment, still shows the person who won it.
