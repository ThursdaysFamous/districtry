Benton County Board of Commissioners

Who won each seat on the Benton County Board of Commissioners at its most recent election, from the Minnesota Secretary of State's results.

These are the people who won each seat on Benton County's Board of Commissioners at its most recent election in the Minnesota Secretary of State's results, each dated to that election. They are election winners, not a list the county keeps up to date, so a seat that has changed hands since, by a resignation or an appointment, still shows the person who won it.
